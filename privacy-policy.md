# AI Journalist Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: [Current Date]*

## 1. Introduction

AI Journalist is a video interview application that helps users create professional-quality video stories using AI-assisted interviewing. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- Video and audio recordings created during interviews
- Speech-to-text transcriptions of your responses
- Story titles and metadata
- Authentication information (email) for account management

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- App usage data
- Device information (operating system version, device model)
- Performance and error data

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Generate AI-powered interview questions
- Create and store your video stories
- Provide speech-to-text transcription services
- Authenticate your account
- Improve our app's performance and features
- Comply with legal requirements

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Local Storage
- Interview segments are temporarily stored on your device
- Completed stories are stored securely in the cloud
- We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data

### 4.2 Cloud Storage
- We use Supabase for secure cloud storage
- All data transfers are encrypted
- Access to stored data is strictly controlled

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- OpenAI API for generating interview questions
- Apple's Speech Recognition for transcription
- Firebase/Supabase for authentication and storage

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy and data handling practices.

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only:
- With your explicit consent
- To comply with legal obligations
- To protect our rights or property

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:
- Access your stored videos and data
- Delete your account and associated data
- Export your video content
- Control camera and microphone permissions
- Opt out of optional features

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email.

## 10. Camera and Microphone Usage

Our app requires access to:
- Camera: For recording video interviews
- Microphone: For recording audio and speech recognition

These permissions are essential for core app functionality and are only used when actively recording.

## 11. Data Retention

- Interview segments are retained until story completion
- Completed stories remain in your account until deleted
- Account data is retained while your account is active

California residents have additional rights regarding their personal information under the CCPA.

## 14. International Users

By using our app, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States and its processing there.

## 15. Compliance

This app complies with:
- Apple App Store Guidelines
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
